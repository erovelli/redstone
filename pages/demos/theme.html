A dark-mode switch for the whole page: data-theme on <html> is light while the lamp is lit and dark while it is out. The choice is stored in localStorage, and the next visit starts from it (or from prefers-color-scheme). This switch and the one in the page header stay in step, each through its own lever. Style the page with :root[data-theme="dark"]. To avoid a flash, set the attribute from storage in a small script in <head>, as this page does.

With auto (<rs-theme auto>Daylight</rs-theme>), a daylight detector drives the lamp and the sky follows the system colour scheme. It is not shown live here because it would take the page away from the header switch.
