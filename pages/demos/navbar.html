Each link is an ender pearl in a stasis chamber. Press one: the chamber drains, the pearl drops onto slime and two pistons fling it up and across the page. The page follows the link when the pearl has left the screen; cancel rs-navigate to route it yourself (the demo below does, and logs it). With reduced motion it navigates at once. Narrower than its wall, it scrolls sideways rather than shrinking the signs.
