A range input. Set min, max and step as on a native one; a comparator shows 16 levels, so up to 16 values get one lamp each and a wider range snaps to 16 evenly spaced values. Arrow keys, PageUp/PageDown and Home/End move books and quills one at a time until the comparator reads the next level; press or drag along the lamps to aim for one.
